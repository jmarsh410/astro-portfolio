## Background

Planet GO! was a website that allowed parents to search for local PBS events that they could take their children to.

## Responsibilities

For this project I was hired as a freelance developer to come in and revamp the existing website. Together with a Project Coordinator, we wireframed and planned a completed vision for the site. A major focus was ensuring that the previously static site was now responsive and viewable on all devices.

## Process

Originally, the site had not been coded to be responsive. Much of my process began with wireframing solutions to take the existing structure of the site and make it respond to its different breakpoints.
